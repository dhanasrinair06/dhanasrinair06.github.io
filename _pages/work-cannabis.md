During my Regulatory Affairs internship at Medicinal Genomics, I tracked the constantly-shifting world of cannabis microbial-testing regulation across all 50 US states.

What I'm proudest of: after spotting a patient-safety gap, I petitioned state regulators to add *Aspergillus niger* — a mycotoxin risk — to their required cannabis testing panels. One state adopted the change. Helping shape a regulation to better protect patients is exactly why I love this work.

The living, state-by-state resource I helped maintain is public [here](https://medicinalgenomics.com/resource/cannabis-microbial-testing-regulations-by-state/).
