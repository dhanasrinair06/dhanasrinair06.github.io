    <p class="work-category__role">During my Regulatory Affairs internship at Medicinal Genomics, I tracked the constantly shifting world of cannabis microbial-testing regulation across all 50 US states.</p>

  <section class="work-category">
    <h2 class="work-category__title">Where I Created Impact</h2>
    <p class="work-category__role">I petitioned state regulators to add <em>Aspergillus niger</em> to their required cannabis testing panels to detect spores and mycotoxin release, thereby ensuring patient safety with regard to medicinal cannabis usage. One state adopted the change. Helping shape a regulation to better protect patients is exactly why I love this work. The living, state-by-state resource I helped maintain is public <a href="https://medicinalgenomics.com/resource/cannabis-microbial-testing-regulations-by-state/">here</a>.</p>
