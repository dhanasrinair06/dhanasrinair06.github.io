During my Regulatory Affairs internship at Medicinal Genomics (MGC), I worked at the fast-moving intersection of cannabis and microbial-testing regulation across the United States.

- Maintained a real-time regulatory compendium of cannabis microbial testing requirements spanning all 50 US states and territories, continuously tracking evolving state-level regulations to keep it current.
- Analyzed nationwide cannabis and hemp microbial assessment trends to support standardized testing discussions and internal decision-making.
- Drafted SOPs to improve compendium update workflows and cross-functional alignment.
- Authored letters urging state regulators to add *Aspergillus niger* to required cannabis testing panels over a mycotoxin patient-safety risk — one state adopted the change.
- Engaged directly with state cannabis regulators to clarify evolving testing methods and answer questions from internal sales, marketing, and regulatory teams.

The state-by-state cannabis microbial testing resource I contributed to is publicly available here: [Cannabis Microbial Testing Regulations by State](https://medicinalgenomics.com/resource/cannabis-microbial-testing-regulations-by-state/).
