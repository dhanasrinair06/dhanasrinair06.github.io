During my Regulatory Affairs internship at Medicinal Genomics, I tracked the constantly shifting world of cannabis microbial-testing regulation across all 50 US states.

Where I created impact: I petitioned state regulators to add *Aspergillus niger* to their required cannabis testing panels to detect spores and mycotoxin release, thereby ensuring patient safety with regard to medicinal cannabis usage. One state adopted the change. Helping shape a regulation to better protect patients is exactly why I love this work. The living, state-by-state resource I helped maintain is public [here](https://medicinalgenomics.com/resource/cannabis-microbial-testing-regulations-by-state/).
